# Project Instructions

These apply to this repo only: code comments, docs, commit messages, UI copy, and generated question text.

# AI Slop & Style Mitigation Directive

## 1. Forbidden High-Frequency Buzzwords & Phrases
Avoid these heavily overused AI tells. Replace them with direct, simple language or omit them entirely.

* **Transition & Introductory Phrases:**
  * "In conclusion..." / "In summary..."
  * "It is important to remember..." / "Crucial to note..."
  * "Furthermore..." / "Moreover..." / "Importantly..."
  * "Delve into..." / "Let's dive deep..."
  * "Testament to..."
  * "Buckle up..." / "Look no further..."
  * "A nuanced understanding..."

* **Cliché Metaphors & Imagery:**
  * "Tapestry" (e.g., "the rich tapestry of...")
  * "Beacon" (e.g., "a beacon of hope...")
  * "Labyrinth" / "Maze"
  * "Landscape" (e.g., "in today's digital landscape...")
  * "Symphony" / "Harmonious balance"
  * "Double-edged sword"
  * "Revolutionize" / "Transformative" (unless backed by specific factual metrics)

* **Verbosity & Filler Verb Choices:**
  * "Utilize" (prefer "use")
  * "Foster" (e.g., "foster a community")
  * "Spearhead"
  * "Elevate" / "Optimize" (when used vaguely)

## 2. Structural & Macro-Level Dead Giveaways
AI writing often suffers from predictable layout patterns that scream "machine-generated." Stop using the following formats:

* **The "Sandwich" Structure (Preambles & Summaries):** Do not provide an introductory paragraph explaining what you are *about to* say, followed by a list, followed by a repetitive conclusion summarizing what you *just* said. Start immediately with the answer.
* **The "Bold Prefix" Colon List:** Avoid formatting every single list item as **Bold Buzzword:** Generic sentence structure. Variate syntax.
* **Hyper-Symmetrical Layouts:** AI tends to make every paragraph exactly 3 sentences or every bullet point exactly 12 words. Vary paragraph and sentence length naturally.
* **"Cheerleader" Closings:** Never end a technical or practical response with motivational, overly positive, or preachy sign-offs (e.g., "Happy coding!", "Remember, the journey of learning never ends!").

## 3. Tone, Perspective, and Rhetorical Anti-Patterns
* **The "On the One Hand" False Equivalence:** Avoid mechanical, overly neutral, or robotic balancing acts that refuse to take a stance on objective technical questions.
* **Polite Sycophancy:** Do not over-apologize ("I apologize for the confusion...", "As an AI, I..."). Be candid, correct the error, and move on.
* **Lack of Pragmatic Context:** AI writes theoretically. Ensure responses account for messy real-world constraints (e.g., budget, time, skill level, depreciation) rather than pristine textbook scenarios.
* **The "Passive Voice Glide":** Avoid hiding the agent of action behind soft passive verbs ("It is often thought that..." or "Mistakes were made..."). Use active, punchy prose.

## 4. Quality Enforcement & Self-Correction Protocols
Before outputs are finalized, pass them through this internal filter:
1. **The Skim Test:** If a human can glance at the text and predict the ending of every sentence based on generic patterns, rewrite it.
2. **The "Who Talks Like This?" Filter:** Read the text internally. If no subject-matter expert would ever say it out loud in a conversation with a peer, strip the corporate veneer.
3. **The Information Density Metric:** If an entire paragraph can be removed without losing core data or actionable steps, delete it.
